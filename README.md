# Enterprise Business Intelligence Analytics Suite (Manufacturing/E-com Domain)

An end-to-end Enterprise Business Intelligence ecosystem connecting custom **Python ETL pipelines**, a **PostgreSQL relational database model**, and a unified **3-Tab Power BI interactive suite** optimizing corporate Inventory, Sales Performance, and HR Lifecycle operations.

---

## 📈 System Architecture & Tech Stack
*   **Data Ingestion & Ingestion Engine:** Python (Pandas, NumPy, SQLAlchemy)
*   **Database Layer:** PostgreSQL Server (Relational structural models)
*   **Analytics & Business Logic:** Power BI Desktop (Advanced DAX, Conditional Formatting, Threshold matrices)

---

## 📊 Core Portfolio Portals

### 📦 1. Smart Supply Chain & Inventory Portal (`/project-1-inventory`)
*   **Business Problem:** Preventing manual stockout tracking, preventing dead capital lock-up, and managing thousands of warehouse rows smoothly.
*   **Technical Implementation:** Engineered a relational database comparing actual warehouse stock levels directly against item-specific minimum safety points. 
*   **Executive Impact:** Implemented a dynamic `Reorder_Status` alerting engine. Shortages flash bold red to notify purchase teams instantly, reducing potential revenue losses and lowering warehousing capital traps.

### 💰 2. Target-Driven Sales Performance Portal (`/project-2-sales`)
*   **Business Problem:** Aligning gross transaction volume metrics with target quotas across multiple distribution regions.
*   **Technical Implementation:** Formulated custom conditional models tracking absolute sales volumes against dynamic 15% quota thresholds using advanced DAX parameters.
*   **Executive Impact:** Built data matrices pinpointing a 40% sales increase inside the North region, giving directors immediate visibility into where to scale marketing capital for maximum ROI.

### 👥 3. HR Operational & Productivity Matrix (`/project-3-hr`)
*   **Business Problem:** Monitoring department execution bottlenecks and tracing employee lifecycle value.
*   **Technical Implementation:** Programmed custom metrics evaluating total upskilling investment (training hours completed) against employee task target outcomes.
*   **Executive Impact:** Pinpoints localized productivity gaps across administrative/logistical wings, allowing senior leadership to scale corporate training resources scientifically.
